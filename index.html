Tickwell: Pill Pal

Reminders for the medicines, exercises and follow-up visits your care team gave you after knee or hip replacement surgery.

Need medical help?

In an emergency, call 911. For health advice from a nurse in British Columbia, call 811 (HealthLink BC). Deaf or hard of hearing: call 711 (TTY relay).

We can't answer medical questions. Please ask your care team or pharmacist.

Common questions

Where is my information stored?

Only on your phone. Tickwell has no accounts and no servers, and we can't see your information.

How do I delete my information?

In Tickwell, go to Settings and tap "Delete all my data". Deleting the app also removes it.

I got a new phone. Where is my plan?

To keep your health information private, Tickwell is left out of iCloud and computer backups, so it starts empty on a new iPhone. Enter your plan again from your labels and care team instructions.

My reminders don't come.

In Tickwell, go to Settings › Notifications and tap "Send a test reminder". Check that notifications are allowed for Tickwell, and that the iPhone isn't on Do Not Disturb or a Focus that hides Tickwell. Reminders can be late or not come, so don't use Tickwell as your only reminder.
